3 Ply Corrugated Boxes

Single-Wall Board · Light Industrial & Retail

3 ply corrugated boxes use a single-wall construction: two flat linerboards sandwiching one fluted layer, giving three layers in total. This is the lightest and most economical corrugated board structure while still offering solid protection for low-to-moderate weight items.

Overview

3 ply corrugated boxes use a single-wall construction: two flat linerboards sandwiching one fluted layer, giving three layers in total. This is the lightest and most economical corrugated board structure while still offering solid protection for low-to-moderate weight items.

We manufacture 3 ply boxes in-house at our Vapi facility, sized to your product rather than a generic catalog size, and can add offset printing or die-cut features on request.

Common Uses
  • E-commerce shipping mailers
  • Retail and gift packaging
  • Small parts and components
  • Short-distance, low-handling shipments

Specifications

Wall TypeSingle Wall (3 Ply)
Layers2 Liners + 1 Flute
